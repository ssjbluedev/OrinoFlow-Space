Overview
What is Orino Flow?
Orino Flow is a desktop app for AI-driven video content production: generate images & video, produce an entire short film with recurring characters using Film Studio, build your own pipelines with the Magic Flow node editor, clone or design AI voices, and finish the cut — all in one app.
Which operating systems does Orino Flow support?
Windows 10/11 and macOS on Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4 chips). There is no Intel Mac or Linux build yet — both are in development.
Do I need my own AI provider account?
Yes. The app calls Gemini, Google Flow / Veo, and ChatGPT through your own account — connect it in Settings and you control your own AI usage and cost. The app does not provide "free" AI generation on your behalf.
Is my video/audio content sent to your servers?
No. Video, audio and subtitle processing happens right on your machine. Orino Flow does not upload this content to our servers. AI image/video/voice generation requests go directly from your machine to the provider you connected (Gemini / Google Flow / ChatGPT), under their own terms.
What is the MCP server, and what is it for?
Orino Flow ships with a built-in MCP server (Model Context Protocol) — it lets an external AI agent or CLI tool control the app remotely: build and run Magic Flow graphs, trigger image/video generation, without opening the app's UI. Toggle it in Settings.
Account & Plans
How does the free trial work?
Every new account gets a 1-day trial with every feature enabled, no card required.
What plans are available?
Monthly, 3-month and 6-month plans — manual renewal. Features are identical across all paid tiers — the difference is trial-vs-paid usage limits, not feature gating.
What payment methods are accepted?
Visa/Mastercard and PayPal. Your plan activates automatically right after successful payment.
How many devices can I use it on?
Each plan allows activation on 1 device at a time. You can remove an old device from the Account screen to sign in on a new one.
What is the refund policy?
See the Terms of Service page — Refund Policy section — for details. Contact support with your order info to request one.
Technical
What happens on first install?
The Setup Wizard installs a "media core" (ffmpeg) and a local AI processing environment for the steps that run on your machine — about a 1.2GB download, with automatic GPU/CPU detection. This only happens once.
Does it work without a GPU?
Yes. The Setup Wizard detects your hardware and falls back to CPU mode if no compatible GPU is found — local processing steps (media core, subtitles, editing) still work fully, just slower.
Where do I connect my AI provider account?
Go to Settings → AI Providers, pick Gemini / Google Flow / ChatGPT / Claude, and sign in or paste an API key. Credentials are stored locally on your machine.
Does the app auto-update?
Yes. Go to Settings → "Check for updates" — if a new version exists, install and restart, no manual installer download needed.
