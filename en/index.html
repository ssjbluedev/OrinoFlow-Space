How it works

Three steps. From idea to finished video.

1
Start from an idea
Type a one-line logline for Film Studio, or drag prompt nodes in Magic Flow — no filmmaking or coding skills needed.
2
AI does the heavy lifting
AI writes the synopsis, breaks it into scenes and shots, creates recurring characters, generates images/video, and dubs the dialogue — running through your own AI provider account.
3
Assemble & publish
Assembly auto-applies cross-fades, burns in subtitles, mixes music — export fast, ready for every platform.
Features

One app.
The full AI video production pipeline.

Image Studio

Generate AI images one at a time or in batch from a .txt prompt list, via Gemini / Google Flow / ChatGPT. Use reference images, with a per-task table for retry, copy-prompt, and preview.

Video Studio

Text-to-video and image-to-video (start/end frame) via Google Flow / Veo. Batch regenerate, upscale, concatenate selected clips, and use reference videos.

Film Studio

From a one-line idea to a finished short film: AI writes the story, breaks it into scenes & shots, creates recurring characters, produces each shot with native Veo dialogue or TTS voice-over, then assembles automatically.

Voice Studio

TTS with Fast and Premium engines. Clone a voice from a 3–10s sample. Design a brand-new voice from a gender/age/pitch/style description. Import/export your voice library.

Video Editor

Built-in editor with a timeline and watermark controls — finish your cut right inside the app, no separate video editor needed.

Job Center

Central queue for every async AI job — image gen, video gen, film production, voice generation — track progress, cancel, retry, and open results as soon as they land.

FAQ

Common questions

What is Orino Flow?
A desktop app for AI-driven video content production: generate images and video, produce entire short films with Film Studio, build custom pipelines with the Magic Flow node editor, and clone or design AI voices — all using AI provider accounts you connect yourself.
Does it need my own AI provider account?
Yes. Orino Flow calls Gemini, Google Flow / Veo, and ChatGPT through your own accounts — you control your own usage and cost. The app itself does not charge per AI generation.
Is my video and audio content uploaded anywhere?
No. Video, audio and subtitle processing happens locally on your machine. Nothing is uploaded to Orino's own servers — only account and license data is stored server-side for login and billing.
What is the MCP server for?
It lets an external AI agent or CLI control Orino Flow remotely over the Model Context Protocol — build and run Magic Flow graphs, or trigger image/video generation, from outside the app.
Which platforms are supported?
Windows 10/11 and macOS on Apple Silicon (M1 and newer). There is no Intel Mac build yet — it is in development.
How does the free trial work?
Every new account gets a 1-day trial with every feature enabled — no credit card required.
