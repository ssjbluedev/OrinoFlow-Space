Privacy Policy

We take your privacy seriously. This document explains what we collect, why, and what rights you have over your data.

Last updated: July 30, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Orino Flow collects the minimum information required to provide the service:

  • Account information: your email address when you sign up with email or Google.
  • Billing information: transactions are processed by a third-party payment gateway (card or PayPal). We do not store your card details.
  • Service operation data: activated devices, subscription plan, and app version — used to enforce plan limits, manage devices, and deliver auto-updates.
Video, audio, subtitles, images, prompts, and any content you process or create in the app are never sent to our servers. Local processing (media core, editing, subtitle work) happens right on your machine.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

Because most of the production pipeline runs through your own AI provider accounts, we do not collect or store:

  • Any video, image, audio, source subtitles, or finished output you create.
  • Prompts, scripts, synopses, or any creative content you enter into Image Studio, Video Studio, Film Studio, or Magic Flow.
  • API keys or tokens for the AI providers you connect — these are stored locally on your machine and never synced to our servers.

3. Your AI Provider Accounts

Orino Flow does not operate its own generative-AI infrastructure. When you connect a Gemini, Google Flow, ChatGPT, or Anthropic Claude account in Settings, every image, video, or voice generation request is sent directly from your machine to that provider, under their own privacy policy and terms. We do not see, store, or process these requests.

4. How We Use Information

Collected information is used to:

  • Authenticate you and manage login sessions.
  • Verify subscription status and enforce device limits.
  • Send account confirmation emails, account notices, and invoices.
  • Deliver automatic app updates.
  • Provide technical support when you request it.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising purposes.

5. Data Storage & Security

Account information is stored on secure infrastructure: passwords are always encrypted before storage, all connections are protected by HTTPS, and login sessions are time-limited and can be revoked remotely when needed.

6. MCP Server

When you enable the MCP server so an external AI agent or CLI can control the app, that connection runs locally on your machine (localhost) — we do not relay or log the content of commands sent through the MCP server.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: update your account information in the app or via support email.
  • Deletion: request deletion of your account and all associated data.

To exercise these rights, contact us via the Contact page or email support@orino.space.

8. User-Generated Content

All content you process and create with Orino Flow (images, video, films, synthesized/cloned voices, subtitles) is produced and stored locally on your machine — we have no access to, do not moderate, and do not store this content.

You bear full responsibility for the content you produce and how you use, publish, or distribute it. See Terms of Service — Section 6 for details.
